About Forge Forward

Empowering Veterans for High-Demand Digital Careers.

Organization Overview

Forge Forward Digital Skills for Veterans is a Texas-based 501(c)(3) public charity dedicated to helping U.S. military veterans successfully transition into civilian careers through technology education, career readiness training, and mentorship. We equip veterans with practical, job-ready digital skills aligned with today's workforce needs.

The Problem

Each year, thousands of transitioning veterans face:

  • ⚠ Underemployment despite strong leadership and discipline
  • ⚠ Limited access to affordable, relevant technology training
  • ⚠ Skills gaps between military experience and civilian tech careers

Without targeted support, veterans are at higher risk of long-term unemployment or low-wage work.

Our Solution

Forge Forward delivers structured, skills-based training programs that prepare veterans for in-demand digital roles, including:

  • ✓ Web and software fundamentals
  • ✓ IT and digital literacy skills
  • ✓ Career readiness, résumé support, and interview preparation
  • ✓ One-on-one mentorship and coaching

Programs are designed to be accessible, practical, and directly aligned with employer needs.

Program Impact

Through our programs, veterans gain:

  • ★ Marketable technical skills
  • ★ Increased employment readiness
  • ★ Improved earning potential
  • ★ Confidence and long-term career stability

Outcomes are measured through participant completion rates, certifications earned, job placement, and wage growth.

Why Forge Forward

  • ● Veteran-led and mission-driven
  • ● Strong governance and compliance practices
  • ● Scalable program model
  • ● Focused on measurable outcomes
  • ● Committed to responsible stewardship of funds

Forge Forward is structured to grow responsibly while maintaining program quality and accountability.
